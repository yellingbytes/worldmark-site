Privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Worldmark (“the app”), developed by Yao Guo, handles information. It is written in plain language.

Summary

Worldmark does not require an account. The developer does not collect your personal data. The app has no analytics, tracking, or advertising. Your visits, notes, and photos stay on your device.

Who is responsible

Worldmark is developed by Yao Guo. Worldmark is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by UNESCO.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or store your passport data, visits, notes, photos, or location. There is no Worldmark account, and the app does not include analytics, tracking SDKs, or ads.

Data stored on your device

All visits, notes, ratings, companions, photos, and other passport information are stored locally on your device. You can export your passport as a JSON file from Profile. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

Location

Location access is optional. If you grant it, it is used only on your device to show nearby World Heritage Sites. Your location is never sent to the developer.

Map tiles

The in-app map loads tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). When those tiles are requested, OpenFreeMap may receive standard request information such as your IP address, in accordance with their policy.

Links to other sites

Some links in the app open external sites, including UNESCO pages and Apple Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and Worldmark is not responsible for their practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@colorplate.app.
