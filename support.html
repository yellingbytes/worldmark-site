Support

Worldmark support.

We would love to hear from you, whether you have found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest a feature. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Contact

App support: support@colorplate.app

Where is my data stored?

All of your visits, notes, photos, and passport data are stored only on your device. Worldmark has no account system and does not upload your data to a cloud service run by the developer.

Can I export my passport?

Yes. Open Profile in the app and export your passport as a JSON file.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes your Worldmark data from the device. Export your passport first if you want to keep a copy.
